### PIX at Omie
#### Multi-bank integration
***
Responsible for developing PIX functionality (sending, receiving and generating charges) within Omie's banking integration, covering multiple financial institutions — Itaú, Banco do Brasil, Santander, Sicoob, Banco Inter, among others. Besides PIX, I also work on generating and settling bank slips (boletos), querying and reconciling bank statements, and electronic payments, always focused on high availability and reliability of financial processes.
#### Technologies

 - Xharbour
 - PHP
 - Grafana
 - Intercon
***
### PIX Direto at Z1
#### Integration with Brazil's Central Bank
***
On Z1's Wallet team — a platform that reached 700 thousand registered users — I worked on the integration with Brazil's Central Bank that enabled **PIX Direto** within the app, expanding the ways users could move money.
#### Technologies

 - TypeScript
 - AWS Lambda
 - AWS DynamoDB
 - AWS QLDB
